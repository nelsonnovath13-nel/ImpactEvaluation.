02 · Foundations

Concept of Consultancy

Core meaning: Consultancy is presented as a professional service or practice in which qualified expertise and specialized knowledge are offered independently to help clients identify problems, make decisions, improve performance and develop or implement appropriate solutions.

The notes define management consultancy as a service provided by an independent and qualified person or persons for identifying and investigating problems concerned with policy, organization, procedures and methods, recommending appropriate action and helping to implement those recommendations.

Consultancy is also described as a professional service in which a qualified expert provides advice, guidance and solutions to individuals, organizations or government to solve problems and improve performance (definition by the International Council of Management Consulting Institutes). It is the practice of offering independent and specialized knowledge to support decision making, planning, implementation and evaluation of activities and projects.

A consultant is a person who gives professional or expert advice (Collins English Dictionary, 2009). The notes caution that the title “consultant” may be used by people without the necessary training, skills or personal disposition. Consultancy should therefore not be understood as advice alone; professional competence is central.

05 · Roles of Consultants

Roles and Models of Consultants

The notes use two models to clarify consultant roles: the Margulies and Raia model and Lippitt's model.

Margulies and Raia: Task-Oriented vs Process-Oriented

Task-OrientedProcess-Oriented
A more autocratic, problem-solving approach.More involved with working with clients, gathering feedback and working with them.
The consultant acts as expert and collects data personally.The consultant takes workers into account and questions them rather than relying only on personal expertise.
The consultant gives an expert opinion.The consultant provides data and allows clients to interpret it.
The consultant makes final recommendations based on data.The consultant develops client learning by having the client take action on the research; the notes describe the process consultant as a teacher.
Concern is focused on the problem.Involves people in the organization and the workings of the research.
Interpretation: The key distinction is the balance between consultant expertise and client participation. The task approach emphasizes expert problem-solving; the process approach emphasizes involvement, interpretation and learning.

Lippitt's Model: Eight Roles

  1. Advocate
  2. Information specialist
  3. Trainer/Educator
  4. Problem solver
  5. Identifier of alternatives and linker to resources
  6. Fact-finder
  7. Counselor
  8. Observer/Reflector

A consultant's contribution can therefore take several forms depending on the assignment, not simply giving a final answer.

07 · Behavioral Skills

Behavioral Skills: Complete Coverage

The notes identify behavioral skills and give deeper discussion for several of them. Technical knowledge alone is therefore not enough; professional behavior also affects consultancy practice.

1. Ability to Care for Others

Caring for others, including strangers, is linked in the notes to sociability and social capital (Wilkinson & Bittman, 2003). Professionals with a duty of care may balance emotional engagement with a degree of “normal splitting” to protect mental health and maintain reliable services (Foster, 2001).

2. Respect for Self and Others

Consultants should explore similarities and differences to discover shared understanding and provide temporary leadership promoting accountability (Mowles, 2009). Respect enhances understanding and service (Brown, 1998), fosters harmonious relationships in culturally diverse groups and promotes equal access to consultancy benefits (Wilson et al., 2009).

3. Quality Consciousness

Identified as a distinct behavioral skill in the notes and should not be omitted or merged into another category.

4. Environmental Awareness

Involves understanding human impacts on the environment and developing knowledge, attitudes and skills to maintain ecological balance. Environmental consultants act as intermediaries between clients and stakeholders and may use conservative consultation methods to minimize risks to desired outcomes.

5. Ability to Abide Principles

Supports ethical and effective service delivery. Adherence to quality management principles can improve performance and client satisfaction, while consultants balance ethical considerations, stakeholder interests and long-term outcomes.

6. Occupational Safety

Consultants should be aware of adopting safety plans, providing insurance coverage and ensuring workers are properly trained to handle tools and equipment.

7. Team-Working Ability

Effective team-based work requires knowledge of organizational design, process management and change facilitation. The notes mention the Teamwork Knowledge, Skills, and Ability (KSA) test as a reliable predictor of effective teamwork in consultancy.

8. Quest for Further Education

Has important implications for consultancy and supports bridging the disconnection between theoretical knowledge and practical application in industry.

9. Determination and Self-Confidence

Play important roles in entrepreneurship and consultancy. They can be developed through training, with novice consultants showing greater gains than advanced counterparts; the notes also refer to favorable feedback from consultees on process- and problem-focused skills.

10. Ability to Work Under Pressure

Identified as a separate behavioral skill and therefore retained as its own concept.

09 · Practice Development

Practice Consultancy Development Strategy

Meaning: A planned approach used by a consulting firm or consultant to improve, expand and sustain consulting services over time. The notes emphasize procedures and process.
1

Define the Current Issues

Conceptualize needs in the market and identify and understand problems, challenges or opportunities requiring consultancy. Examples include low employee productivity, poor financial performance and declining customer satisfaction. Issues should be clearly defined before solutions are developed.

2

Specify Target Consulting Projects

Evaluate the environment, understand the degree of organizational change and select targets.

3

Recognize Strengths and Weaknesses

Consider assumptions on abilities and determine the pathway.

4

Design the Consultancy Assignment

Develop an action plan, agree with clients, understand organizational change and match project design with client needs.

5

Consultancy Implementation and Monitoring

Take action, involve the client and understand hidden factors.

13 · Market

Concept of the Consultancy Market

The consultancy market is the environment where consultancy services are demanded and supplied. It also refers to the current state and trends within the consulting industry, shaped by economic factors, client demand, technological advancement and industry-specific challenges. It includes consultancy firms, independent consultants, governments, NGOs and private organizations and changes according to economic and technological conditions.

Conditions of the Consultancy Market

  • Growth and demand: moderate to strong global growth, especially in digital transformation.
  • Rapid technological change and economic uncertainty: conditions associated with increased demand.
  • Increased competition: clients are more value-conscious, compare costs and expect measurable results.
  • Technology disruption: AI and data analytics are redefining consulting services; firms increasingly use technology to augment insights and automate repeatable tasks (Gartner, 2024).
  • Government regulation
  • Economic conditions

Challenges

  1. High competition among firms.
  2. Economic instability affecting consultancy budgets.
  3. Rapid technological changes.
  4. Ethical issues and corruption.
  5. Difficulty maintaining skilled professionals.

16 · Taxation

Taxation in Consultancy Services

Consultancy firms are required to comply with tax laws. The notes identify income tax, VAT, withholding tax and corporate tax as common taxes, while emphasizing that obligations differ according to national regulations.

General Handling

  • Maintain proper bookkeeping and financial records.
  • File tax returns regularly.
  • Hire accountants or tax professionals.
  • Separate business and personal finances.
  • Understand changes in tax laws.

Tanzania Context in the Notes

  • Register with the Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA) to obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN).
  • Understand obligations including income tax, VAT and WHT.
  • File returns and keep records: the notes state monthly VAT returns, quarterly provisional income tax returns and annual income tax returns.
  • Seek licensed tax practitioners or accountants due to regulatory complexity and to reduce audit risks.
  • Failure to comply may result in penalties, interest charges or audits.

Importance of Tax Compliance

  • Prevents legal penalties and fines.
  • Improves organizational credibility.
  • Builds trust with clients and government authorities.
  • Supports financial transparency.
  • Helps firms qualify for contracts and tenders.
17 · Review

Review and Model Answers

These model answers follow the review questions and use only the provided course materials.

1. Consultancy, market, firm establishment and communication in M&E

Consultancy provides independent, specialized professional knowledge for solving problems and improving performance. The market is where services are demanded and supplied and is shaped by demand, technology, economic conditions and competition. Establishing a firm requires specialization, market research, planning, legal registration, licensing/tax registration, marketing and professional registration. In M&E consulting, communication supports stakeholder understanding, accurate data collection, clear presentation of findings, management of expectations, trust and collaboration.

2. Handling taxation and importance of compliance

The notes emphasize bookkeeping, regular filing, professional support, separation of business and personal finances and awareness of changes in tax laws. Tanzania-specific notes mention TRA/TIN, income tax/VAT/WHT, the stated returns and records, professional help and consequences of non-compliance. Compliance also supports credibility, trust, transparency and eligibility for contracts and tenders.

3. Steps when conducting consultancy

The strongest source-supported process is: define current issues; specify target consulting projects; recognize strengths and weaknesses; design the consultancy assignment; and implement and monitor. The notes connect these stages with market needs, environment, organizational change, action planning, client agreement and involvement, and hidden factors.

4. Consultancy in project planning, monitoring and evaluation

The notes support consultancy for designing data collection tools and techniques, conducting baseline/midline/endline evaluation, supporting accountability, learning and improvement, analyzing data and preparing technical reports.

5. Communication skills in M&E consulting

Active listening improves stakeholder understanding; interview skills support accurate data; presentation clarifies findings; negotiation manages expectations; and good communication strengthens trust and collaboration.
